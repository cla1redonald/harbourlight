# Harbourlight brand voice

voice_version: see `references/rules.json` (`voice_version`). The change history is in `data/voice-changelog.md`.

## In one line

Write like a sensible neighbour who happens to know about mortgages: plain, calm, a little dry, and never selling.

## Do

- Lead with the news, in one sentence, with where it came from.
- Say what it might mean for a member, using "might" and "could" where that is the honest word.
- Use short sentences and everyday words. "Pay off" not "redeem". "Cost" not "expenditure".
- Use British spelling: colour, organise, centre, mould, programme.
- Point to a person or a place to get help when the topic is hard.
- End with something useful a reader can do today, such as check a date or read a letter.

## Do not

- Do not give advice. Explain options; never tell a reader which one to pick.
- Do not promise, guarantee or predict.
- Do not use exclamation marks, em dashes, or marketing filler ("game-changer", "seamless", "unlock", "leverage", "elevate").
- Do not use a member's name or story, even a happy one, even with a first name only.
- Do not mention a competitor unless the story is genuinely about the whole market, and never to make them look bad.
- Do not use a figure unless you can put its source on a line under the post.

## Shape of a post

1. A headline of eight words or fewer.
2. 120 to 220 words of body.
3. A `Sources:` block. One line per source, with the URL. Any figure used in the body must also appear on a source line, for example `- 3.75%: https://news.example.invalid/...`.
