# Products and approved claims

Each product has exactly one approved line. A post may mention at most one product, and only by using its approved line word for word. Any other sentence that names a product is an unapproved claim (rule H2).

The machine-readable copy of this list lives in `references/rules.json`. If the two ever disagree, `rules.json` wins, because that is what the checker reads. Changing either needs a named approver (see `commands/voice-apply.md`).

| Product | Short name | Approved line |
|---|---|---|
| FixedFive Remortgage | FixedFive | The FixedFive Remortgage fixes your monthly payment for five years. |
| Overpayment Planner | Overpayment Planner | The Overpayment Planner shows how an extra payment would change your mortgage term before you make it. |
| HomeAssist Emergency Cover | HomeAssist | HomeAssist Emergency Cover is an optional add-on to our home insurance that arranges help with emergencies such as a burst pipe or a broken boiler. |
| Steady Ground support line | Steady Ground | The Steady Ground support line gives members who are worried about payments a named contact to talk through their options. |
| Harbour App | Harbour App | The Harbour App lets members check their balance, make overpayments and update their details. |

## Notes

- Steady Ground is a **signpost**, not a sale. It is the only product that may appear in a post about a sensitive topic, and only to point people to help.
- None of the approved lines contains a rate, a price or a saving. That is deliberate: figures go stale and need a source.
