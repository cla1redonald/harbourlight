# Watchlist: five content themes

The market-research skill looks for one story per theme on each run. A theme marked **sensitive** can produce a post, but that post can never be auto-passed: a person must read it (rule H7).

| Id | Theme | What we look for | Sensitive |
|---|---|---|---|
| T1 | Rates and mortgage costs | Base rate decisions, fixed-rate deals ending, remortgage timing | No |
| T2 | Weather and home damage | Storms, flooding, frozen pipes, insurance claims after bad weather | No |
| T3 | Money worries and payment support | Arrears, missed payments, hardship, bereavement and mortgages | **Yes** |
| T4 | Scams targeting homeowners | Fake texts, cloned firms, conveyancing fraud | **Yes** |
| T5 | Energy and running a home | Boiler servicing, insulation grants, damp and mould, running costs | No |

## Out of scope

Car insurance, pensions, investments, crypto-assets, business lending, and anything outside the UK. A story outside these themes is skipped and the reason is written in the research log.
