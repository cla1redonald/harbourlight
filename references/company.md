# Harbourlight Mutual

Everything in this file is invented. Harbourlight Mutual is not a real company, and the Conduct Standards Authority is not a real regulator.

## Who we are

Harbourlight Mutual is a UK mutual owned by its members. It services residential mortgages and home insurance for a few thousand households, mostly in Fife, the Lothians and Tayside. It has no shareholders, so it answers to members rather than investors.

It is regulated by the **Conduct Standards Authority** (CSA), an invented regulator used throughout this repo. The CSA expects financial promotions to be clear, fair and not misleading, and expects firms to treat members in difficulty with particular care.

## What we post

Short posts (120 to 220 words) for the Harbourlight members' newsletter and social channels, reacting to something in the news that matters to people with a mortgage or a home to insure. A post explains the news, says what it might mean for a member, and at most mentions one product using its approved line.

## What we never post

Advice, promises, predictions about rates, anything naming a member, anything about a competitor's misfortune, and anything about arrears, bereavement, vulnerability or scams that has not been read by a person first.
