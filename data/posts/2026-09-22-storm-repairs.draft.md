# Storm repairs, done quickly

After the storm earlier this month, Douglas Thomson, 72, a Harbourlight member since 1994, told the Kingdom Gazette his roof was patched within two days of phoning. "I phoned on the Sunday and a roofer was up the ladder on the Tuesday," he said.

It is a good reminder of what to do when the weather turns. Take photographs of any damage before you start tidying up, because insurers usually ask for pictures when you make a claim. Keep receipts for anything you pay for to stop the damage getting worse, such as a tarpaulin or an emergency call-out.

If rain is getting in, a temporary fix is fine while you wait for a proper repair. Do not climb onto a roof yourself, however tempting it is on a dry afternoon.

Mr Thomson said he had been worried about rain getting into the loft before the repair was done. Most people would be.
