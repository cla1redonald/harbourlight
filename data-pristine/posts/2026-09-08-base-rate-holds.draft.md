# Base rate holds again

The central bank kept its base rate at 3.75% this month, the third decision in a row without a change, The Forth Ledger reports. Three of the nine committee members wanted a cut, so the vote was closer than the headline suggests.

What might this mean for you? If you are on a fixed rate, nothing changes until your deal ends. If you are on a variable rate, lenders said their pricing already took the hold into account, so a change to your monthly payment is unlikely to follow from this decision alone.

The next decision is due on 5 November. Nobody knows which way it will go, and we will not guess.

If your fixed deal ends in the next six months, it is worth finding the end date on your annual statement now, so that you have time to look at your options without a deadline pressing. The FixedFive Remortgage fixes your monthly payment for five years.
