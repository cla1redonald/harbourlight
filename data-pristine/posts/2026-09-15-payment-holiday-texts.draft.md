# Fake payment holiday texts

Police in Tayside are warning about text messages that offer a mortgage payment holiday and ask you to click a link to confirm your details, the Tay Valley Courier reports. The texts use lenders' names and logos, so they can look convincing.

Police said genuine lenders do not ask for bank details by text. If a message like this arrives, do not click the link!

If you are unsure whether a message is really from us, call the number printed on your mortgage statement rather than any number in the message. The same goes for any lender: the number you already have is the one to trust.

If you have already clicked a link or shared details, tell your bank straight away. Acting quickly gives them the best chance to stop a payment going out.

Scams like this work by making you feel hurried. A genuine lender will always give you time to check, and will not mind if you hang up and call back.
